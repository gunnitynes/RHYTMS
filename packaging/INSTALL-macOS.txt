RHYTMS - installing on macOS

1. Quit Ableton Live (or your DAW).

2. Copy the plugins:
   RHYTMS.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   RHYTMS.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (Audio Unit, optional)
   RHYTMS.app        ->  /Applications/                          (standalone, optional)

   (~ is your home folder. In Finder: Go > Go to Folder... and paste the path.)

3. macOS quarantines downloaded plugins and hosts then silently skip them.
   Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/RHYTMS.vst3
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/RHYTMS.component

4. Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins
   - turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (and "Use Audio Units v2" for the AU)
   - press Rescan (hold Option while clicking Rescan for a full rescan)

5. In the browser look under Plug-Ins > VST3 > gunnitynes > RHYTMS.
   It is an audio effect: drop it on an audio track.
